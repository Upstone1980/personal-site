About

Founder · Operator · Investor — mission-critical technology in Australia

I'm a senior technology executive based in Perth, Western Australia, with over twenty years of experience building, leading, and investing in companies that deliver mission-critical communications and ICT infrastructure. My career spans the natural resources sector, enterprise IT, systems integration, and private mobile networks — across Australia and globally.

I'm currently Director of Public and Private Networks at BAI Communications, leading the delivery of private 5G, LTE, and mission-critical network solutions for mining, energy, rail, government, and transport clients. I'm also Non-Executive Director and co-founder of Vitalis Solutions, a Brisbane-based master systems integrator serving Australia's healthcare and education sectors.

My approach is grounded in operational reality. I've run P&Ls, led teams through acquisitions, built companies from scratch, and designed networks that can't fail. I bring the same discipline to board-level governance that I bring to a network design review — rigour, accountability, and a focus on what works in the field.
