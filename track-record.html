Operating Leadership

Scaling ICT Services Nationally — Titan ICT

Advisory & Systems Integration P&L Ownership Managing Director

Context

Joined Titan ICT to lead its growth and prepare the business for sale. The company operated across Western Australia and Queensland, focused on the Advisory and Systems Integration markets, delivering critical telecommunications solutions to the natural resources sector.

My role

Managing Director with full P&L accountability. Led all functions: sales, delivery, operations, finance, and team building. Drove the go-to-market strategy, won and retained major accounts, built a national capability, and positioned the business for a successful exit and transition.

What changed

Delivered consistent year-on-year revenue and margin growth across the full tenure, expanding the client base across resources, government, and enterprise. Built a high-performing team culture, developed proprietary delivery frameworks, and maintained growth momentum right through to a successful business sale and transition.

Board-relevant lessons

Scaling a business towards a successful exit requires simultaneous management of culture, capital, clients, and execution risk. The decisions that most affect long-term value — on talent, positioning, and financial discipline — demand both operational clarity and strategic patience. Understanding how a business is actually built and sold makes you a more effective board director.

Change Leadership Culture Growth

My role

Senior leader responsible for maintaining performance and culture through uncertainty. Setting direction, communicating with transparency, making difficult resource and structural decisions, and sustaining client confidence during periods of internal change.

Investment Themes

Founder and largest personal investment in Vitalis Solutions, a specialist ICT systems integration business serving the healthcare and education sectors. These sectors have structural demand for digital transformation and long-term funding tailwinds. Active board involvement across strategy, capital, and growth.

Digital Infrastructure

Energy Transition & Grid Assets

Grid-linked renewables, battery storage, and transmission infrastructure. The energy transition represents one of the largest infrastructure investment cycles in Australian history. I focus on assets with long-term contracted revenue and grid-critical positioning.

AI-Enabled Platforms

Early-stage and growth-stage platforms applying AI to industrial and infrastructure sectors. Focus on businesses with genuine defensible data advantages and real operational value creation — not AI as a feature veneer.

Natural Resources & Real Assets

Long-duration resource assets with strategic positioning — particularly those tied to the energy transition value chain, including battery metals and critical minerals in Western Australia.

Capital Allocation Principles

Across all investment activity: patient capital with a multi-year horizon; thesis-driven entry; downside discipline before upside optimism; focus on businesses and assets with structural advantages and barriers to replication. Avoidance of narrative-driven investments without underlying cash flow or asset value.
